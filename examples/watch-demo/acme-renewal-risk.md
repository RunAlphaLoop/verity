# Acme Freight — Renewal Risk Assessment (Q3)

**Account:** Acme Freight Co (account:acme-freight)
**CSM:** Jordan (user:jordan)
**Owning team:** Sales (group:sales)
**Stage:** Negotiation — decision expected this quarter

## Summary

The Acme Freight renewal is **at risk**. The customer pushed back hard on
pricing during the last renewal call and asked for a multi-year discount.
We revised the quote from **$48k to $61k** after the pricing review, and a
competitor is rumored to have quoted around **$52k** for the same scope.

## Renewal risk factors

1. **Price sensitivity.** The $61k revised quote is a ~27% increase over the
   prior $48k. Acme's procurement team flagged the jump directly.
2. **Competitive pressure.** A rumored $52k competitor quote undercuts us.
   Our differentiation is the live dispatch integration and the 95% on-time
   tracking SLA — lead with those, not price.
3. **Deal-desk floor.** The internal renewal floor is $58k/yr. Anything below
   that needs deal-desk approval; do not verbally commit under $58k.

## Recommended play

- Reinforce the dispatch integration (live since March) and the tracking SLA
  as switching-cost anchors.
- Offer a multi-year term to bridge the price gap rather than discounting the
  annual number below the $58k floor.
- Target a signed renewal before quarter close.

**Overall renewal risk: HIGH** — driven by price gap + active competitor.
